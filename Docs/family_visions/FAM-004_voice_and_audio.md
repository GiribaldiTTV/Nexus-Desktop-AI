# FAM-004 Voice And Audio Vision

## Purpose

This family vision records durable product direction for voice availability, audio path direction, voice diagnostics, truthful disabled/degraded copy, and future persona/audio capability boundaries.

## Vision Summary

Voice and audio should reinforce ORIN as an assistant presence without making the product feel unreliable when voice is unavailable, disabled, degraded, or future-gated.

## Accepted Direction

- Preserve truthful voice-disabled and degraded-state communication.
- Keep assistant/persona identity aligned with `Docs/nexus_vision.md` and `Docs/orin_display_naming_guidance.md`.
- Validate voice/audio behavior through explicit runtime or waiver proof, not implied availability.
- Keep future richer audio/persona behavior USER-gated.

## Implementation Boundaries

- This vision does not admit microphone capture, always-listening behavior, voice/Core runtime sync, or private assistant tooling.
- Verify voice/audio privacy, reliability, disabled/degraded states, and the visible behavior a user can actually exercise.
